## 1. Overview

Orbit Rush ("the App") is a single-player arcade game. The App does not
require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, contacts,
location, camera, or microphone, and has no chat or social features.

## 2. Data stored on your device only

Game progress — level, XP, unlocks, daily streak, best score, missions,
and rebirth multiplier — is stored **locally on your device** using the
WebView's local storage. It never leaves your device, and we cannot
access it. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads served by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC). To serve and
measure ads, Google may collect:

- **Advertising ID** (a resettable, device-level identifier)
- **IP address** and coarse, IP-derived location
- **Device information** (model, OS version, screen size, language)
- **Ad interaction data** (impressions, clicks, ad-load diagnostics)

This collection is performed by Google's SDK, not by us, and is governed
by Google's Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.
How Google uses data from apps that use its services:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

## 4. Consent (GDPR / UK GDPR / US state laws)

In regions where consent is required (e.g. EEA/UK), the App shows the
**Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)** consent form on first launch,
before any ads load. You can choose personalized or non-personalized
ads, or (where offered) decline. You may change your choice at any time
from the App's settings <!-- TODO: confirm you expose the

Depending on your region you may have rights to access, delete, or
object to processing of personal data. Ad-related data is processed by
Google — exercise choices via the UMP consent form, Android's ad
settings, or <https://adssettings.google.com>. For anything else,
contact us at the email above.
